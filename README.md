# AssignMe

**A personalized AI academic workspace for university and college students.**

## What AssignMe Is

AssignMe is an AI-powered academic workspace that helps students research, write, and structure academic work according to their field of study.

Unlike a general AI chatbot, AssignMe knows who the student is, what they study, and what they are working on — and personalizes every response around that context:

**Student + Discipline + Project = Personalized Academic Experience**

## Who It Is For

University and college students — e.g. Nursing, Law, Engineering, Computer Science, Accounting — working on:

- Assignments
- Seminars
- Research projects
- Final-year projects
- Literature reviews
- Academic writing and citations

## The Problem It Solves

General AI tools give generic answers, forget project context, and risk invented references. Students have to re-explain their course, level, and requirements every time.

AssignMe solves this with context before generation:

- Remembers profile: course, university, department, level, citation style
- Activates discipline-specific Academic Intelligence (terminology, structure, sources, standards)
- Remembers project context: topic, objectives, sources, requirements, progress

## Main Features

- **Account & Academic Profile:** name, email, course, university, department, level, citation preference
- **Discipline Intelligence:** Nursing, Law, Engineering, etc., each with its own academic guidance
- **Project Workspaces:** separate workspace per task with memory, saved work, and resume-anytime
- **Core Tasks:** topics, objectives, outlines, assignments, seminars, research, writing help, references
- **Research & Citations:** academic source discovery, basic verification, APA 7 / MLA / Harvard / Chicago / Vancouver / IEEE support
- **Freemium:** free basic help + paid advanced research, full projects, and extensive references

## How Students Use It

1. **Sign up** with name, email, password
2. **Build academic profile** — course, university, department, level, citation style
3. **Open personal workspace** — e.g. "Welcome back, Theresa. Your Nursing Academic Intelligence is ready."
4. **Choose a task** — Assignment, Seminar, Research, Topic, Objectives, Writing, References
5. **Create or continue a project** — work gradually, not all at once
6. **Get AI assistance** grounded in profile + discipline + project context
7. **Save and return** — all work stays in the account
