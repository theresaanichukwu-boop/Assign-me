# **ASSIGNME**

## **Product Requirements Document**

**Product:** AssignMe  
**Product Type:** AI-powered academic workspace  
**Primary Users:** University and college students  
**Business Model:** Freemium  
**Core Idea:** A personalized, discipline-specific AI academic assistant.

---

# **1\. PRODUCT OVERVIEW**

AssignMe is an AI-powered academic platform for university and college students.

It helps students with:

* Assignments  
* Seminars  
* Research  
* Final-year projects  
* Literature reviews  
* Academic writing  
* Topics and objectives  
* References and citations  
* Academic research

Unlike a general AI chatbot, AssignMe is designed to understand **who the student is, what they study, and what academic task they are working on**.

The student's experience is personalized through their account, academic profile, selected course and individual projects.

The core product flow is:

**Sign Up → Academic Profile → Course → Academic Intelligence → Task → Project Workspace → AI Assistance**

---

# **2\. THE MAIN PRODUCT IDEA**

AssignMe should not feel like:

> "Ask an AI anything."

It should feel like:

> **"This is my personal academic workspace."**

Every student has their own account.

Their account contains their academic information and their projects.

For example:

**Theresa's AssignMe**

**Course:** Nursing  
**University:** University of Nigeria  
**Level:** Final Year  
**Citation Style:** APA 7

AssignMe then uses this information to personalize the student's experience.

---

# **USERS AND ROLES**

AssignMe will have different user roles with different levels of access and responsibilities. The system should clearly separate **human users** from the **AI Academic Intelligence** used to support students.

## **1\. Student**

The **Student** is the primary user and customer of AssignMe.

Students create personal accounts using their name, email address and password. After signing up, they create an academic profile containing information such as their course, university, department and academic level.

Students can:

* Create and manage their personal academic profile  
* Select their course/field of study  
* Access the Academic Intelligence for their discipline  
* Create assignments, seminars, research projects and other academic tasks  
* Create and manage projects  
* Generate topics and objectives  
* Conduct academic research  
* Find and manage academic references  
* Generate and check citations  
* Get academic writing assistance  
* Upload relevant academic documents  
* Save and continue previous work  
* Manage their account and preferences  
* View their usage and subscription  
* Upgrade from the free plan to a paid plan

Each student's information and projects should be private and accessible only to the student and authorized AssignMe staff when necessary for support or administration.

---

## **2\. Admin**

The **Admin** manages the overall AssignMe platform.

Admins can:

* Manage student accounts  
* Manage user access  
* View platform activity and usage  
* Manage courses and disciplines  
* Manage Academic Intelligence configurations  
* Manage free and paid plans  
* Manage subscriptions and payments  
* Manage platform content  
* Review system issues  
* Manage reports and analytics  
* Configure platform settings  
* Manage other staff roles and permissions

Admin access should be protected because it provides access to sensitive platform and user-management functions.

---

## **3\. Academic Intelligence Manager**

The **Academic Intelligence Manager** is responsible for creating, maintaining and improving the discipline-specific Academic Intelligence.

This role ensures that each discipline has appropriate academic instructions and standards.

For example:

* Nursing Academic Intelligence  
* Law Academic Intelligence  
* Engineering Academic Intelligence  
* Computer Science Academic Intelligence  
* Accounting Academic Intelligence

The Academic Intelligence Manager can:

* Create new discipline intelligence  
* Update existing discipline intelligence  
* Define discipline-specific terminology  
* Define appropriate academic structures  
* Define appropriate research approaches  
* Define recommended source types  
* Add discipline-specific academic guidance  
* Review the quality of AI responses  
* Update academic requirements as needed

This role does **not** represent a human professor account. The Academic Intelligence is an AI system configured and maintained by AssignMe.

---

## **4\. Support Staff**

**Support Staff** help students with account, payment and general platform issues.

They can:

* View support requests  
* Assist with account problems  
* Help with subscription issues  
* Help students navigate the platform  
* Escalate technical or academic-intelligence issues  
* Manage customer support tickets

Support Staff should have limited access and should not automatically have access to all student academic content.

---

# **AI ACADEMIC INTELLIGENCE ROLES**

The Academic Intelligence is not a human user role. It is the AI layer assigned to a student's selected discipline.

Each Academic Intelligence should have its own:

* Academic knowledge  
* Terminology  
* Research standards  
* Writing expectations  
* Discipline-specific instructions  
* Appropriate source types  
* Academic structures  
* Task-specific guidance

Examples include:

**Nursing Academic Intelligence**  
Acts as the academic intelligence for Nursing students.

**Law Academic Intelligence**  
Acts as the academic intelligence for Law students.

**Engineering Academic Intelligence**  
Acts as the academic intelligence for Engineering students.

The student's selected course determines which Academic Intelligence is activated.

Therefore, the system should follow:

**Student Account → Academic Profile → Course → Academic Intelligence → Task → Project**

This structure allows AssignMe to provide a personalized academic experience while keeping human user permissions separate from the AI systems powering the product.

**Finding academic evidence.**

* Government publications

* University publications  
* Official reports

# **17\. REFERENCE VERIFICATION**

When AssignMe provides a reference, it should verify available information such as:

* Author  
* Publication year  
* Article title  
* Journal  
* Volume  
* Issue  
* Pages  
* DOI  
* URL where appropriate

Sources should be clearly identified as verified or requiring verification.

AssignMe must never present an invented source as a real academic reference.

**Student Account → Academic Profile → Course → Academic Intelligence → Task → Project**

This structure allows AssignMe to provide a personalized academic experience while keeping human user permissions separate from the AI systems powering the product.
